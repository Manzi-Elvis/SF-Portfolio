89.5 FM ABOUT

I am a seasoned Social and Behavior Change (SBC) and Strategic Communications professional with over 15 years of experience in media and communications, having served in senior roles across government ministries, international NGOs, and media organizations.

My expertise lies in designing and implementing SBC interventions that drive meaningful behavior change. I utilize evidence-based approaches such as Audience-Driven Design and the Socio-Ecological Model to uncover behavioral norms, barriers, incentives, and solutions that support sustainable development outcomes.

Most recently, I served as the SBC and Program Communications Advisor for the USAID Tunoze Gusoma (Schools and Systems) Activity, implemented nationwide by FHI 360 (lead) in partnership with Save the Children International and Florida State University. In this role, I led the development and rollout of SBC and communications strategies that contributed to significant improvements in foundational literacy across Rwandan public and government aided schools.

"Bridging policy, community engagement, and strategic communications for diverse interventions."

I have a track record in successful designing and execution of strategic communications, stakeholder engagement, event management, media relations, and multi-sectoral communication strategies. With deep knowledge of Rwanda's education policies, the East African Community Treaty, regional integration protocols, gender and social inclusion policies, and domestic media regulations, I am capable of bridging policy, community engagement, and strategic communications for diverse interventions. I have successfully led national awareness campaigns, facilitated public-private partnerships, and driven impactful narratives that elevated development outcomes.

I began my career in journalism, working for over five years as a journalist and Editor-in-Chief at Radio Rwanda under the Rwanda Broadcasting Agency (formerly ORINFOR). This media foundation strengthened my storytelling, audience engagement, and media relations skills, which continue to inform my work today.

As a Safeguarding Focal Point at FHI 360, I also trained staff and program participants on safeguarding policies to prevent any form of abuse, harassment, or discrimination within work environments and program implementation.

92.7 FM APPROACH

Two frameworks, one aim: change that sticks.

Every intervention starts by locating where a behavior actually forms — inside a person, or the systems around them — and designing for that level specifically.

Audience-Driven Design

Interventions are built from the audience outward — starting with their real barriers, incentives and lived context, rather than a message handed down and hoped for.

Socio-Ecological Model

Behavior is shaped at five nested levels. Effective SBC work names which ring a barrier actually lives in, then intervenes there — not everywhere at once.
