Background

Why this matters

Teen mothers in Gakenke District face school dropout, stigma, discrimination and exclusion from health insurance. Community norms often treat teenage pregnancy as shameful — limiting education, healthcare and opportunity.

Rwanda has strong policies supporting girls' education and reintegration of adolescent mothers, but gaps in implementation persist at community level.

8%
of girls aged 15–19 in Rwanda have experienced pregnancy
Source: Rwanda Demographic & Health Survey, 2025

Key drivers

  • Gender inequality and poverty
  • Harmful social norms & weak protection
  • Limited awareness of support services
Together, we build stronger families and stronger communities.

Collective action

Everyone has a role

Creating a supportive environment for teen mothers requires every part of the community to play its part.

Local leaders

Champion inclusive policies and services at sector and cell level.

Schools & teachers

Promote safe, welcoming and non-discriminatory learning environments.

Health & social workers

Provide respectful, confidential and accessible services.

Families

Offer acceptance, encouragement and practical day-to-day support.

Communities & peers

Challenge stigma, promote inclusion, and stop discrimination.

Your part

What you can do

School leaders & teachers

“Every girl deserves a second chance.”

Create an inclusive school. Support reintegration pathways, and make sure returning mothers are welcomed, not whispered about.

Parents & caregivers

“Her future doesn't end with motherhood.”

Support your daughter and grandchild. Help her understand the education and health pathways still open to her.

Students

“Support, don't stigmatise.”

Avoid bullying and exclusion. Welcome classmates who are teen mothers back into school life.

Community members

“Supporting teen mothers strengthens us all.”

Challenge gossip and judgement when you hear it. Encourage, don't isolate.
